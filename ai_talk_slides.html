AI can produce:

  • confidently wrong answers

5 / 20

What not to paste

Do not include

  • passwords, secrets, or credentials
  • production access details
  • sensitive customer / member / employee data
  • confidential internal information
  • regulated data
  • anything your organization prohibits

Review carefully

  • authentication / security logic
  • infrastructure changes
  • production SQL
  • destructive automation
